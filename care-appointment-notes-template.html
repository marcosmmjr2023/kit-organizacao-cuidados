Care appointment notes

A useful appointment record separates what you observed, what you want to ask and what the professional actually confirmed. Use these two pages with the person receiving care to prepare priorities, capture instructions accurately and give every follow-up a named owner and date.

Notes are not medical instructions. Do not use this template to diagnose, interpret results, change medication, calculate a dose or invent an instruction. Read back important details during the appointment and follow the current written guidance from the appropriate professional. Seek urgent or emergency help when needed.

Prepare and record without guessing

  1. Choose priorities together. Ask the person what they most want discussed and who they want involved.
  2. Describe observable facts. Record what happened, when and how often—not a diagnosis or conclusion.
  3. Bring the current sources. Use the latest professional medication list, requested documents and relevant contact details.
  4. Read back instructions. Ask for clarification and mark an item confirmed only when the appropriate professional confirms it.
  5. Assign the follow-up. Give each agreed next step an owner, due date and status.

Check before leaving or ending the call

  • The person’s questions were addressed or left visibly pending
  • Important instructions were read back
  • No medication change was inferred from these notes
  • Each next step has an agreed owner
  • Contact directions came from the professional
  • The next appointment or review is confirmed or marked pending

Privacy and safe use

Appointment notes can contain sensitive information. Record only what is necessary, ask the person before sharing it, store completed pages securely and remove outdated copies. Do not include passwords, financial credentials or full identification numbers. Keep clinical records in the channel recommended by the care service.
