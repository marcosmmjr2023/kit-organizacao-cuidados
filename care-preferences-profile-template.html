Care preferences profile

A useful profile describes how a person prefers to communicate, participate and move through the day. Complete these two pages together with the person receiving care, record only what they agree to share, and review the profile whenever routines or support change.

Preferences are not permission or a clinical plan. Ask the person directly whenever possible. Never use this profile to infer consent, capacity, diagnosis, treatment or emergency instructions. Follow the current care plan and confirmed professional guidance; contact the appropriate professional or emergency service when needed.

Create the profile with the person

  1. Start with their words. Ask what helps them feel heard, respected and in control.
  2. Describe observable preferences. Write “prefers one question at a time,” not a label or assumption.
  3. Separate choice from support. Record what the person does independently and what help was explicitly agreed.
  4. Keep alternatives visible. Preferences can change; ask again rather than treating this sheet as permanent permission.
  5. Share minimally. Include only details needed by the people providing the agreed support.

Privacy and safe use

This profile may contain personal information. Keep only the minimum needed, store it securely, and ask before sharing it. Remove obsolete copies after an update. Do not include passwords, financial credentials, full identification numbers, diagnoses or detailed clinical records.
