What you receive

Access to the installable English PWA, an English ZIP archive, and the Android APK. Simple view reduces the interface to four large, predictable actions while retaining add, edit, and delete actions for appointments, medicines, and contacts. The full view remains available for caregivers.

Two reminder layers: the PWA displays a persistent visual and sound alert while it is open and exports times to the device calendar. The Android APK uses local system notifications after permission is granted, including when CuidaLocal is closed and without internet.

Important limits: browsers do not guarantee future timers after a PWA is fully closed, so use the device calendar or Android APK for those cases. Android notifications still depend on the device being on, permission remaining active, and manufacturer battery policy. CuidaLocal organizes information; it does not diagnose, prescribe, suggest doses, or replace professionals, a dedicated alarm clock, or emergency services.

Install and use reminders

English PWA

  1. Open the English app and use “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
  2. On the dashboard, select “Enable alarms”. The internal alert works while the PWA remains open.
  3. Select “Add to device calendar”, open the downloaded file, and import the “CuidaLocal” calendar for reminders while the PWA is closed.

Android APK

  1. Download the APK and authorize installation if Android asks.
  2. Open CuidaLocal, select “Enable alarms”, and grant notification permission.
  3. Do not put CuidaLocal under extreme battery restriction. Reminders are local and work without internet.
  4. Create periodic backups through “Data and backups”.
