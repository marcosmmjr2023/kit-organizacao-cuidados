Collect less, protect more. Do not write passwords, access codes, full account numbers or unnecessary identity and health details on these sheets. Store completed pages securely, remove stale copies and share only what is needed and agreed. These are administrative organizers—not clinical advice, proof of consent or emergency instructions.
