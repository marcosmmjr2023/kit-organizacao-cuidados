Home care supplies inventory

Running out and buying duplicates are both easier to avoid when quantities and ownership are visible. These two pages separate consumable supplies from reusable or borrowed equipment, then turn real shortages into assigned follow-ups.

Count what is present; do not prescribe what is needed. Record only items already in an agreed care routine or current professional plan. An inventory does not decide which products to use, how often to use them or whether equipment is safe for a person.

How to inventory care supplies

  1. Check physically. Use quantities and dates seen on the package instead of copying an old list.
  2. Separate stock types. Keep disposable supplies apart from reusable, rented or borrowed equipment.
  3. Use an agreed reorder point. Copy a threshold already confirmed by the household or supplier; do not invent one from this sheet.
  4. Assign each follow-up. A purchase, refill, return or service request needs an owner and due date they accepted.
  5. Date every review. Keep the latest sheet and securely dispose of stale copies.

Safety and privacy boundaries

Do not use this inventory to choose products, change prescribed quantities, substitute supplies, assess equipment safety or delay professional help. Follow current manufacturer, supplier and professional instructions. Do not record account passwords, payment details, door codes, full identity numbers or unnecessary health information.
