Medical appointment transport checklist

Getting to an appointment can involve more than booking a ride. These two pages help the person receiving care and their caregiver confirm timing, accessibility, willing support, requested items and the return trip—while leaving unknowns visibly open.

Plan with the person, not for them. This checklist organizes travel logistics only. It does not decide whether travel is medically safe, select a transport provider, guarantee accessibility or replace instructions from the care service. Contact the service directly about clinical or access requirements and use emergency services when urgent help is needed.

Confirm the journey without assumptions

  1. Confirm the appointment first. Check the date, arrival time, entrance and service contact using the current appointment notice.
  2. Ask about preferences and access. Record only support the person wants and requirements confirmed with the venue or transport provider.
  3. Name a support person only after they agree. Confirm the time, meeting place and specific responsibility.
  4. Plan both directions. Keep the outward trip and return trip separate; mark any unbooked leg as open.
  5. Carry only what is requested. Use current documents and professional records, and avoid unnecessary sensitive details.

Privacy and safe use

Do not write passwords, door codes, full identity numbers, payment credentials or clinical details that are not needed for the journey. Store completed sheets securely and remove stale copies. Do not use this checklist to start, stop, delay or change medication, food, treatment or professional instructions.
