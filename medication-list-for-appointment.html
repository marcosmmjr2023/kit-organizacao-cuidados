Medication list for a healthcare appointment

Use this administrative checklist to gather confirmed information and questions before an appointment. It is not a prescription, dose calculator, or a substitute for professional advice.

Important: do not start, stop, change, or interpret medicines, doses, timing, or frequency using this page. If something is unclear, contact the responsible clinician or service. For urgent situations, follow the emergency plan you have received.

1

Start with the most recent confirmed source

Gather the latest written instructions, prescription labels, medication packaging, and the previous appointment list. Note the date of each source. When sources disagree, write “confirm” rather than choosing one yourself.

2

Copy confirmed details, not guesses

For each item, record its name as shown on the label or instruction, the listed form or strength, the guidance received, the clinician or service, and when the entry was last updated. Leave uncertain details marked for confirmation.

3

Include items that are easy to miss

Bring information about vitamins, supplements, topical products, herbal products, and items used only when needed. A professional can decide what is relevant to the appointment.

4

Turn uncertainty into questions

Write clear questions such as “Is this still the current instruction?” or “What should be recorded if a dose is missed?” Keep clinical decisions with the professional.

Before leaving for the appointment

  • Date the list.
  • Mark questions without filling gaps from memory.
  • Keep relevant documents together.
  • Bring a responsible contact name if appropriate.
  • Save an updated copy after the appointment.

Protect private information

Medication and health information is sensitive. Avoid publishing the list in groups, sharing more than necessary, or storing copies without access control. If you use a digital copy, keep backups and decide who needs access.
