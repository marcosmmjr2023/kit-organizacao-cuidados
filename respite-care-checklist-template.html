Respite care checklist

Time away is easier to protect when temporary coverage is explicit. These two pages help the person receiving care, the usual caregiver and the respite caregiver confirm the period, agreed responsibilities, practical access and a handback plan.

Confirm care; do not assume it. Involve the person receiving care wherever possible. A task belongs on this checklist only when the respite caregiver has accepted it and has the authority, information and preparation needed. Use the current care plan and professional instructions; this sheet does not authorize treatment changes.

How to prepare temporary coverage

  1. Define the exact window. Record arrival, departure and who is responsible before and after it.
  2. Confirm each responsibility. Separate accepted tasks from open gaps or items handled by someone else.
  3. Share only what is needed. Explain access in person where possible instead of copying codes onto the sheet.
  4. Name escalation contacts. Include when to contact each person or service, using the current care plan for clinical questions.
  5. Agree on handback. Choose a time and channel for objective updates and unresolved follow-ups.

Safety, consent and privacy

Do not use this checklist to diagnose symptoms, change medicines, invent emergency thresholds or assign a task beyond someone's authority or preparation. Follow the current care plan and contact the appropriate professional or emergency service when needed. Do not write passwords, door codes, full identity numbers or unrelated health details; store and dispose of completed sheets securely.
